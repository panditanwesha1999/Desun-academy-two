About the program

This is not a referral scheme. This is a distribution network.

“The DESUN Business Education Partner Program (BEPP) is a decentralised student acquisition network, powered by people who already have students around them.”

We stop depending on ad budgets and start building distribution nodes. Every coaching centre, every career counsellor, every educator becomes a growth engine for DESUN and earns seriously while doing it.

No office required

Operate from anywhere: your home, college or existing business. No infrastructure investment needed.

We handle everything else

Counselling, admissions, training and placement are all done by DESUN. You refer, we convert.

Earn on every admission

Fixed commission per student who joins, whether it is a crash course or a professional program.

Real-time partner dashboard

Track referrals, admission status and payouts in one place.

From application to first commission in 5 simple steps

The process is fast, simple and completely free to start. Certified Partners are onboarded within 48 hours.

    Step 01

    Fill the application form

    Complete the short application below. Tell us about your background, your network size and how many referrals you expect per month. Takes under 3 minutes.

  2. 2
    Step 02

    Partner onboarding call

    Our Partner Manager will call you within 24 hours. We will walk you through the program, help you choose your tier (Open Affiliate or Certified Partner) and answer every question you have.

    Within 24 hours of applying
  3. 3
    Step 03

    Sign the partner agreement

    A simple, transparent agreement covering commission rates, payment timelines and your responsibilities as a DESUN authorised partner. Signed digitally.

    Digital signing via email

    Step 05
