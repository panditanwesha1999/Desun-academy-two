Beware of imposters!

Desun Academy does not promise a job or an interview in exchange of money. Fraudsters may ask you to…

  • pay a registration, training or “security” fee to get a job.
  • share OTPs, bank details or ID documents before an interview is confirmed.
  • move the conversation to unofficial chat apps or personal email addresses.

If something looks wrong, report it to admin@desunacademy.in.
