Why We Started

When we expanded hiring at DESUN Technology Private Limited, we faced a major challenge—finding candidates with the practical skills and industry knowledge required for real-world IT projects.

During one hiring drive involving B.Tech, MCA, M.Tech, and BCA graduates, nearly 99% of candidates were rejected because they lacked the practical experience and job-ready skills companies were looking for. This made us realize that the gap between traditional education and real industry requirements had become a serious challenge.

At the request of many aspiring candidates, DESUN Academy was established with a clear purpose—to bridge this growing skill gap and make learners industry-ready.

Founded by experienced professionals from DESUN Technology, our courses focus on practical, job-oriented learning through live projects, industry-standard tools, internships, portfolio building, and career grooming. With mentors bringing 9+ years of real-world experience, we help learners develop the skills, confidence, and experience needed to succeed in today’s competitive job market.

Our goal is simple: to create skilled, confident, and work-ready professionals—not just certificate holders.
